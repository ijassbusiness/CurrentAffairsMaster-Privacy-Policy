Delete Your CurrentAffairsMaster Account

CurrentAffairsMaster provides a simple process for users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

Send an email to ijassbusiness@gmail.com from the email address associated with your CurrentAffairsMaster account.

Please use the subject line CurrentAffairsMaster Account Deletion Request and include the email address used for your account.

What will be deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete the CurrentAffairsMaster account and personal information associated with that account, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

This may include account/profile information, quiz progress, bookmarks, preferences, and other personal data associated with the account.

What may be retained

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or satisfy legitimate security and legal obligations. Any retained information will be limited to what is reasonably necessary and will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Deletion requests

We will process valid deletion requests after verifying that the request is associated with the account owner. If additional information is needed to verify the request, we may contact you by email.
