Privacy Policy for CurrentAffairsMaster

Effective date: August 28, 2026

CurrentAffairsMaster is an educational application for practicing current-affairs questions, quizzes, and related learning content. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may process, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the app, CurrentAffairsMaster may process account information such as email address, user identifier, name, profile information, and authentication information; app and learning data such as quiz attempts, scores, answers, progress, bookmarks, and preferences; push-notification token and related device/platform information when notifications are enabled; language and display preferences; and information processed by Google AdMob when advertisements are displayed. The current advertising implementation requests non-personalized ads.

2. How We Use Information

  • create and authenticate user accounts;
  • provide quizzes, results, progress tracking, bookmarks, and profile functionality;
  • save user preferences;
  • send notifications when enabled;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and operate the application; and
  • display advertisements through Google AdMob.

3. Authentication and Service Providers

CurrentAffairsMaster uses Supabase services for backend functionality and authentication. Google authentication may also be used when a user chooses Google sign-in. The app uses Expo notification services for push notifications and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising.

4. Advertising

CurrentAffairsMaster displays advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may process information in accordance with Google's applicable privacy policies and terms. CurrentAffairsMaster's current advertising implementation requests non-personalized ads.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, including Supabase, Google authentication services, Expo notification services, and Google AdMob, as applicable.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information used by the application. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Data Retention

Account and learning information may be retained while needed to provide the application's services and maintain your account and progress. Certain information may also be retained where necessary for security, legal, or operational purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

CurrentAffairsMaster is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for the purpose of creating accounts. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

9. Your Choices

You can choose supported authentication methods, enable or disable available notification preferences, and use the app's available settings. You may also contact us regarding questions about your personal information.

10. Third-Party Services

Third-party services used by the app have their own privacy policies. Users should review the policies of relevant providers, including Google and Supabase, for information about their independent processing practices.
